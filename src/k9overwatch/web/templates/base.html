How to use K9 Overwatch

1

Report

Click Report a lost pet in the nav to submit a lost or found pet. Add a photo and location — it helps a lot.

2

Search

Browse the Map or Pets directory. Use filters to narrow by status, species, and timeframe.

3

Match

Check Matches to see potential pairings. Create an account to contact the person who reported a matching pet.
