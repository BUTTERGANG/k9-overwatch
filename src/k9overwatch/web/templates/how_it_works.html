How K9Overwatch Works

We know how stressful it is when a pet goes missing — or when you find one that needs to get home. Let us help bring them back together.

1

📢 Report a lost or found pet

Tell us about the pet — name, breed, color, where you last saw them, and any special features. Upload a photo so others can recognize them faster. It only takes a couple of minutes.

🤝 Get matched & reunited

Our matching engine automatically compares lost and found reports by breed, color, location, and distinctive features. When a potential match is found, you'll see it highlighted on the map and in your Matches page. Reach out through our privacy-preserving contact system — no emails revealed until you're ready.
